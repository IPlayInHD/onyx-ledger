CRA Document Guide · 2024

Every document the CRA may need — and how ONYX gets it for you.

Below is a comprehensive list of the records the Canada Revenue Agency may require or use for a personal tax return. To minimize manual effort, ONYX groups every input into three sources — so most of it never needs to be uploaded by hand.

● CRA auto-fill

Retrieved from the CRA

With your consent, ONYX can pull tax slips, Notices of Assessment, and carryforward amounts directly through the CRA's Auto-fill my return service.

● Import

From third parties

Imported from banks, brokerages, payroll providers, accounting software, crypto exchanges, and e-commerce platforms.

● Upload / enter

Provided by you

Receipts, invoices, mileage logs, medical expenses, donations, and rental or business records you upload or enter.

Available directly from the CRA

With the taxpayer's authorization, a modern platform can retrieve many slips and records automatically via Auto-fill my return, rather than asking you to upload them:
