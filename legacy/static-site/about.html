About

An independent tool that helps you understand your taxes.

Onyx Ledger is an independent educational service. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). It does not file your taxes.

Onyx Ledger — powered by ONYX Intelligence — reads the tax information you provide, computes an estimate of your federal and provincial tax position, scores your Tax Health, and explains, in plain language, the credits and deductions you may be eligible for. The goal is education: helping Canadians understand their taxes and legally reduce them, not replacing a licensed professional or the CRA's own services.

What it does — and doesn't

  • Does: compute and reconcile your tax position using the CRA's published method, score its reliability for your data, find potential deductions and credits, explain the reasoning, and help you plan.
  • Doesn't: transmit your return to the CRA (that requires NETFILE certification) or replace a licensed professional's advice.
  • Every audit is a reconciled, professional-grade analysis with a stated confidence level. Confirm your specifics with the CRA or a licensed tax professional before you file.

Your data

We take privacy seriously and ask only for what's needed to produce your estimate. In this demo build, your information stays in your own browser and is never uploaded. Read the full Privacy notice.
