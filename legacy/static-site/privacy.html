Privacy

Your information is yours.

In this demo build, your account and every figure you enter are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is uploaded to a server, sent to us, or shared with anyone — including the CRA. Clear your browser data, or use the “Delete my account” button, and it's gone.

What we ask for

Only what's needed to produce your estimate: an email and passcode to hold your local demo profile, and the tax figures you choose to enter (income, deductions, credits). You decide what to provide.

What we never ask for

  • Your Social Insurance Number (SIN). Onyx Ledger does not need it and you should never enter it here.
  • Banking logins, card numbers, or government credentials.
  • Real documents in the demo — sample data and manual figures are enough to see how it works.

How it's handled

  • Demo (this site): stored in your browser only, never transmitted.
  • Hosted app version: data is encrypted in transit and at rest, you can export or delete it anytime, and it is never sold.
  • No advertising trackers. No selling of personal data. Ever.

Not the CRA

Onyx Ledger is an independent educational service, not a government website, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Canada Revenue Agency. It does not file your taxes. See About.
