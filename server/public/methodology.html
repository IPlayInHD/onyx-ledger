Methodology & verification

How ONYX calculates — and how you can check it.

Trust should be earned, not asserted. This page documents exactly how the engine turns your figures into an estimate, the sources behind every constant, how the calculation is verified, and where it deliberately simplifies. Nothing here is a black box.

Onyx Ledger is not CRA-certified filing software and does not file your taxes. In Canada, software that transmits a return to the CRA must pass NETFILE certification — an annual program with the CRA's own test cases. This tool produces educational estimates to help you understand and plan. Always confirm with the CRA or a licensed professional before you file.

The calculation, step by step

Step 1 — Income

Total income

Employment, self-employment, interest, pension and other income are summed. Eligible and non-eligible dividends are grossed up (×1.38 / ×1.15); 50% of a capital gain is included.

Step 2 — Deductions

Net & taxable income

RRSP, FHSA, union dues, child care, moving and employment deductions reduce total income to net income, used as the basis for tax (a documented simplification — see limitations).

Step 3 — Federal tax

Brackets minus credits

Progressive federal brackets are applied, then non-refundable credits (basic personal amount, CPP/EI paid, the Canada employment amount, tuition, medical above the 3% threshold, age, pension, spousal, and donations) are subtracted at 15%, along with the dividend tax credit. Quebec residents receive the 16.5% federal abatement.

Step 4 — Provincial tax

Provincial brackets, credits, surtax

Each province/territory's brackets and credit rate are applied. Ontario's surtax and health premium are included. Provincial credits mirror the shared amounts.

Step 5 — Result

Refund or balance owing

Federal + provincial tax is your total income tax. Subtract tax already withheld or paid to get your estimated refund or balance owing, plus marginal and average rates.

2024 figures are treated as verified. 2025 federal figures are verified against the CRA — including the July 1, 2025 rate cut that lowered the bottom federal rate to 14%, giving a blended 14.5% for the 2025 tax year (so 2025 non-refundable credits are valued at 14.5%). 2025 provincial figures are indexed estimates pending verification against each province's published amounts.

How the engine is verified

  • Reference cases: the engine is checked against hand-derived expected federal, provincial and total tax for representative filers (e.g., Ontario $60,000, Alberta $50,000, British Columbia $100,000). The arithmetic matches the CRA's published method.
  • Self-check on demand: the /api/verify endpoint re-runs those reference cases live, so you can confirm the deployed engine still matches — see the status box above.
  • Property tests: automated tests confirm tax is monotonic in income, marginal ≥ average, no negative tax or credits, and identical inputs always produce identical outputs, across every province and a range of incomes.
  • Deterministic: the engine is pure — the same inputs always yield the same result, with no hidden state.

Documented limitations

  • Net income is used as a proxy for taxable income (no loss carryovers or specialized deductions).
  • Provincial dividend tax credits and some province-specific amounts are approximated.
  • Image OCR requires an OCR provider; the demo reads structured entries and text.
  • Benefit estimates (GST/HST, Canada Carbon Rebate, CCB) use simplified formulas.
  • It is an estimate — not a filed return, not tax advice, and not CRA-certified.

Want authoritative numbers?

Use the CRA's own tools: canada.ca — Canada Revenue Agency, the Auto-fill my return service through NETFILE-certified software, or a licensed tax professional. Onyx Ledger is designed to help you understand those numbers, not replace them.
