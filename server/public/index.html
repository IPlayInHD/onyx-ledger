08 · Questions

Answers before you upload a thing.

Is ONYX actually accurate?
Every calculation follows current Canadian tax rules, and each recommendation traces back to the specific rule behind it. A built-in audit checker flags errors and risks before you file, so you can verify the reasoning rather than take it on faith.
Can I really file my return with it?
ONYX prepares a complete, optimized, filing-ready return and exports it in the formats you need. You submit it through approved filing channels — and ONYX tells you exactly when it’s ready and whether anything is still missing.
Is my financial data safe?
Yes. Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, your data stays yours to export or delete at any time, and we never sell it. Security is one of our five founding principles, not an afterthought.
Is it genuinely free to start?
Yes — no card required. The free tier includes AI Tax Chat, a refund estimator, your Tax Health Score, and year-round tips. You only pay when you file a return ($29.99) or upgrade for year-round planning.
Do I still need an accountant?
For most simple-to-moderate returns, ONYX handles everything an accountant would — for a fraction of the cost. For more complex situations, it makes working with a professional far easier, and can refer you to one when it’s the right call.
Which documents does it support?
T4 and T5 slips, T2202 tuition forms, donation receipts, investment statements, medical receipts and reports, income statements, and more. Upload what you have — ONYX reads it and tells you what’s still outstanding.
Is ONYX affiliated with the CRA?
No. ONYX is an independent platform. Our calculations follow CRA rules, but we are not a government service and are not endorsed by the Canada Revenue Agency.
