# <ENTRY>

BLOCKED / PARTIAL

> A blocker reported with evidence is a successful outcome. Silently widening
> scope to avoid one is not.

## Exact blocker

One precise sentence. Name the missing authority, the forbidden change, or the
contradiction — not a general area of difficulty.

## Classification

One of: PRODUCTION_DEFECT / DESIGN_DEFECT / COMPATIBILITY_DEFECT /
PRIVACY_DEFECT / SECURITY_DEFECT / TEST_DEFECT / GATE_DEFECT /
PERFORMANCE_DEFECT / POLICY. See `defect-taxonomy.md`.

## Evidence

What was measured or read that establishes the blocker — queries run, files
inspected, counts observed. A blocker asserted without evidence is a guess.

## Authority / source inventory

Which authorities and sources were checked, and what each reported. For a
missing historical source, state per family whether a frozen authority exists.

## Why implementation cannot safely continue

What proceeding would require inventing, inferring, or fabricating, and what
would be wrong about the result.

## What would be required to unblock

The narrowest change that would resolve it — a governed decision, a new sealed
source, an approved version bump, an explicit policy call.

## What was NOT changed

Confirm the tree is unchanged, or list precisely what was written and why it was
safe.
